## Release and study decision

The paper's defensible claims concern agreement with generator-informed references on a controlled pilot, the scoped modular-arithmetic correction, and one external development observation. Before reporting a transfer advantage, collect runtime-qualified positive cases and independently adjudicated node references on held-out groups. Report coverage, candidate recall, matched multi-candidate ranking, and the uncertainty at the group level.

The public folder includes the frozen checksummed evidence, corrected paper, replay tools, and the development counters with source previews and source-code identifiers removed. It excludes the raw PIE archives, the sealed held-out manifest, legacy splits, and local notes or build caches. The frozen and corrected manuscripts remain preserved as evidence. Code and manuscript publication terms should be confirmed before making the repository public.

The panel checked saved results, source paths, and the compiled manuscript. The packaging check replays the saved evidence and runs the released Linux fixture checks. GPU training, fresh profiling of held-out submissions, and redistribution-rights review remain outside the verified scope.
