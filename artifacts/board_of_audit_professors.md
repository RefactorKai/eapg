# Board of Audit Professors

Independent review of the frozen EAPG Phase 1 artifact, 9 November 2026

## Board verdict

**Preserve the pilot as a controlled localization study. Correct the fast-side scaling evidence and narrow the claims before submission.** The board reproduced Table IV from the saved predictions and verified the stated fold quarantine. Abstract EAPG's 98/100 stands as a benchmark score. Its two-hit lead over the refined deterministic ranker depends on one related group. The board also confirmed signed overflow in fast programs from two mechanism families.

Three simulated academic reviewers worked in separate agent contexts and inspected the frozen files independently. The chair cross-checked selected findings and ran fresh C++ probes. This audit covers the saved predictions, data construction, implementation, and compiled manuscript. It did not rerun GPU training or evaluate human-written submissions.

## Reviewer 1 — Dataset and quantitative evidence

**Verified.** The pilot matches the SHA-256 in the evaluation log. Reaggregation reproduced all 14 Table IV rows, including Top-1, Top-2, and multi-candidate scores. The saved logits reproduce the 1,200 learned rankings. Rebuilding the declared quarantine from the generator, canonical source keys, and local Qwen tokenizer reproduced 29 source groups, 23 merged groups, and five folds of 20 pairs. Across 700 prompts, the longest prompt and target use 498 tokens. All 100 reference nodes survive the pruned slice. The mean slice has 1.90 nodes and the mean reduction is 88.97%. There are 34 singleton and 66 multi-candidate slices. [Frozen pilot](scope_isolated_pilot/pilot100_results.json) · [Evaluation log](scope_isolated_pilot/node_only_ablation/evaluation_results.json)

**Major — The neural margin depends on group weighting.** On the multi-candidate pairs, binary-tagged Abstract EAPG (`6b-slice`) scores 64/66 and the refined ranker scores 62/66. All four Abstract-only wins (`PAIR_033`–`PAIR_036`) are in the same A1.4 group (`P_PIE_19` and `P_PIE_20`). The ranker's two wins (`PAIR_006` and `PAIR_008`) are in two other groups. Equal weight per merged group reverses the ordering across the full pilot to 93.48% for Abstract and 95.65% for the ranker. Excluding the A1.4 group from scoring gives 94/96 and 96/96 respectively on the saved predictions. Report the discordant groups with the pair-weighted scores. Reserve general claims of neural benefit for untouched data. The manuscript already discloses that the ranker was refined on the pilot. [Evaluation log](scope_isolated_pilot/node_only_ablation/evaluation_results.json) · [Validity discussion](paper_sections_and_refs.tex#L305)

**Major — The definition of a template matters.** The documented source fingerprint preserves numeric literals. An audit sensitivity check that also normalized numeric literals reduced source groups from 29 to 13 and found 16 fingerprints spanning folds, involving 39/100 pairs. For example, `PAIR_001` and `PAIR_003` match under this broader key and sit in different folds. The declared split checks passed. The separate legacy JSONL holdout has exact template overlap affecting 4/11 test cases and is outside the frozen run. For claims about unseen template families, group by generator lineage or a preregistered broader key and report sensitivity to that choice. ## Reviewer 2 — Pipeline and measurement

**High — Fast-side recurrence profiling uses undefined signed arithmetic.** The 13 A2.1 memoized and 12 B.3 tabular fast programs store a recurrence with base values of one in signed `long long`. At `N=92`, its result is 12,200,160,415,121,876,738, above the signed 64-bit limit. Fresh `g++ 15.1.0 -O2` builds with `-fsanitize=signed-integer-overflow -fsanitize-trap=signed-integer-overflow` completed at `N=91` and trapped at `N=92` and `N=256` for all 25 programs. The 25 retained instrumented binaries reported `CLEAN_COMPLETION` when replayed at `N=256`. Qualify the affected fast exponents and paired scaling claims. If those claims are retained, rerun the affected scaling evidence on defined arithmetic with an output oracle and per-scale traces. [Memoized fixtures](../pipeline.py#L1942) · [Tabular fixtures](../pipeline.py#L2279) · [Scale ladder](../pipeline.py#L678)

**Verified — Node-only inputs use slow-side profiles.** The test prompt uses the slow AST and slow counters and stops before the reference token. Fast source and generator-known scope help derive the reference label, and both sources inform fold grouping. Table IV measures agreement with those constructed labels. Separately, the chair matched the generator's sources to the frozen data, compiled 200 uninstrumented binaries, and got byte-identical stdout in all 236 pair-size comparisons. These cover all 100 pairs at `N=16` and `N=24` plus all six A1.4 pairs at sizes 1, 2, 3, 8, 32, and 64. The comparisons establish agreement for those generated inputs. [Reference and profile construction](../pipeline.py#L2556) · [Prompt and target construction](../pipeline.py#L3669)

**Major — The candidate selector needs external failure tests.** A probe with assigned exponents produced no symptom for a single quadratic loop at the top of `main`. A second probe admitted a guard-variable write after the inner loop even though a later assignment overwrites it before its next use. Describe the implementation as a scoped def/use heuristic. Before external testing, account for missing symptoms and empty slices, test reaching definitions and top-level loops, reuse the canonical preprocessed AST for ranker node IDs, and address the 26-node limit of single-letter labels. Isolate external compilation and execution, bound captured output during execution, and check exit status independently of the counter dump. [Symptom selection](../pipeline.py#L2342) · [Candidate selection](../pipeline.py#L2409) · [Ranker reparse](../pipeline.py#L3116) · [Execution wrapper](../pipeline.py#L573)

## Reviewer 3 — Manuscript and interpretation

**Major — Separate representation from decoding restrictions.** All-node graph variants still receive slice tags. The “Raw-Code” baseline sees labeled 26-character macro-node previews. Its 66/100 is a preview-baseline result. As an audit diagnostic, applying the same slice mask to saved logits changes Top-1 from 66 to 73 for the fine-tuned preview baseline, 69 to 79 for the hotspot baseline, and 58 to 74 for the static graph baseline. These are post-hoc decoding results. A stronger comparison would include full-source and preview baselines with matched decoding. Abstract EAPG outperformed Lexical EAPG at 80 training pairs per fold on this pilot. A learning curve and separate identifier and preview controls would test a sample-efficiency explanation. [Prompt construction](../pipeline.py#L3250) · [Table IV](scope_isolated_pilot/node_only_ablation/table_ablation.tex)

**Major — Qualify the detector and taxonomy claims.** Table II's 100% work detection counts the presence of a symptom on positive cases. Work-symptom Top-1 is 82/100. The symptom already equals the reference for every A2 and B pair. On the 18 remaining pairs, Abstract scores 16/18 and the refined ranker 14/18. Include negative workloads and report false alerts before generalizing the detection result. Table I's joint threshold matches 99/100 labels, exactly the same as the absolute edit-count cutoff alone. `PAIR_006` is the Track A1 exception with four changed macro-nodes. Present the threshold as descriptive. Attribute the Table III reduction to the full pruned-slice construction, which changes candidate eligibility as well as monotonic-step pruning. [Detector calculation](../pipeline.py#L2709) · [Taxonomy](paper_sections_and_refs.tex#L140) · [Slice construction](../pipeline.py#L2418)

**Clarifications for Tables III and IV and the case study.** The work-only profiler's 94.1% search reduction in Table III counts the one emitted symptom. Table IV's 0.0% encodes the absence of an explicit slice constraint. Define both conventions. Constrained Top-2 is automatic for the 82 pairs whose reference-containing slice has at most two candidates. The four `std::find` examples are a selected subset of 11 A1.3 pairs. Lexical EAPG with binary tags solves 8/11, and the deterministic ranker solves 11/11. Use those examples to illustrate the two exponents and identify the full-container-size weight as a cost proxy. [Table III](paper_sections_and_refs.tex#L241) · [Table IV](scope_isolated_pilot/node_only_ablation/table_ablation.tex) · [Case study](paper_sections_and_refs.tex#L293)

**Editorial — Correct the bibliography before the next compile.** Import the version-of-record PIE author list and link. Restore Nicolas Wicki and the published author order in the Extra-P citation. Match the EvalPerf author order to the COLM paper. [Local bibliography](references.bib#L10) · [ICLR proceedings](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/ce65173b994cf7c925c71b482ee14a8d-Abstract-Conference.html) · [ACM proceedings](https://doi.org/10.1145/3437801.3441613) · [COLM paper](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=IBCBMeAhmC)

## Board advice in order

1. **Issue a versioned manuscript correction.** Disclose the overflow, narrow Table II and the taxonomy claims, label the preview baselines precisely, and report group sensitivity. Keep the original evidence with the correction. The five-page PDF contains the current Table IV. The adjacent LaTeX build files are from an earlier version and should be separated from a release bundle.
2. **Package an independently rerunnable release.** Pin source, compiler, dependencies, model and tokenizer revisions. Save per-scale execution status, counters, fit diagnostics, and the provenance of each reference and prediction. Reproduce the affected fast-side scaling evidence on a defined input domain if the manuscript relies on it.
3. **Evaluate external data under a fixed protocol.** Sample human-written problems before looking at predictions, preregister input generators and eligibility, and obtain blinded reference judgments with room for multiple valid nodes. Report coverage, candidate recall, multi-candidate accuracy, and paired results by problem or template group. Separate any learning-curve experiment from the held-out evaluation.
