# Scalar-frame feasibility panel

> **Execution Status (17 November 2026):** Automated static, CFG, token-mapping, and finite-slope funnel executed in [`scalar_intake_feasibility.json`](scalar_intake_feasibility.json) via [`audit_scalar_intake.py`](audit_scalar_intake.py) (`5/11` eligible specs across `4` mechanisms; see [`RESEARCH_RECORD.md`](RESEARCH_RECORD.md)).

The scalar comparator needs independently authored programs with one `int main()`, integral local state, and the supported scalar I/O. Public sequence definitions provide tasks that fit that interface. An author still needs to supply a scalable workload, natural reversible interventions, and controls. The designs below are development candidates. Their four-slot eligibility and the yield of distinct confirmed families are unmeasured. [Prospective protocol](PROSPECTIVE_SYNTHETIC_PROTOCOL.md)

## Development candidates

The 16 specifications below form ten candidate groups before provenance and template merging. Give independent authors the task definitions so their implementations, generators, and edits originate independently. These sketches record the feasibility hypotheses for the study team.

| Task and source | Candidate contrast and unresolved question |
| --- | --- |
| Binary weight and its prefix sum. [A000120](https://oeis.org/A000120) · [A000788](https://oeis.org/A000788) | Try exact binary-block aggregation versus summing weights value by value. Test whether independently authored edits can target either the local advance or the upstream limit. |
| Partitions into at most three parts. [A001399](https://oeis.org/A001399) | For each nonnegative `c` in `a + 2b + 3c = N`, count `floor((N-3c)/2)+1` choices directly or enumerate them. The number of terms is linear and the number of choices is quadratic. A whole-workload count and two eligible edit sites still need verification. |
| Lattice points on a circle or inside its disk. [A004018](https://oeis.org/A004018) · [A000328](https://oeis.org/A000328) | Compare a monotone retained boundary with a fresh scan for each column. Review the connection to the existing cursor-reset experiment before allocating a family. |
| Fibonacci and Pell recurrences. [A000045](https://oeis.org/A000045) · [A000129](https://oeis.org/A000129) | Use an explicitly adapted modular workload. Compare scalar recurrence jumps with unit updates or reuse the state across an increasing sequence of queries. Merge shared recurrence scaffolding. |
| Divisor counts and their prefix sum. [A000005](https://oeis.org/A000005) · [A006218](https://oeis.org/A006218) | Exact quotient blocks and unit quotient updates differ by half an asymptotic order. Verify the finite-range band separately before counting any positive design. |
| Lower and upper Wythoff sequences. [A000201](https://oeis.org/A000201) · [A001950](https://oeis.org/A001950) | Investigate exact integer threshold searches with different bounds and advances. Prove equivalence and check all intermediate integer ranges. Keep the complementary sequences together. |
| Step-two Josephus survivor. [A006257](https://oeis.org/A006257) | Investigate exact elimination by power-of-two blocks versus unit updates. Check that the proposed edit preserves the survivor and review ancestry shared with binary-prefix cases. |
| Count of 3-smooth numbers up to a bound. [A003586](https://oeis.org/A003586) | Compare scalar enumeration by powers of two and three with membership testing for every candidate. A natural reversible intervention and separate count implementation are still needed. |
| Totients and their prefix sum. [A000010](https://oeis.org/A000010) · [A002088](https://oeis.org/A002088) | Investigate factorization versus explicit coprimality tests. The aggregate operation counts and finite-range slope depend on the workload. |
| Minimum half-surface area for an integer-sided box of sufficient volume. [A400004](https://oeis.org/A400004) · [Original credit](https://oeis.org/wiki/A400K_Winners) | For fixed positive `x` and `y`, the optimum uses `z = ceil(N/(xy))` because `xy + (x+y)z` increases with `z`. A valid bound on the other dimensions, equivalent single-site edits, and the complete count remain open. |

## Intake and measurements

1. Recruit independent authors for a small development panel. Record specification and aliases, dated origin and contributors, usage rights, generator lineage, and reuse of substantive code. Screen the public development history for overlap. Keep the PIE draw sealed and require no Aizu, AtCoder, CodeNet, or PIE lineage in the proposed specifications or artifacts. Familiar mathematics can have semantic overlap even when code and generators have independent origins. Treat the panel and known template descendants as exposures for future primary-frame quarantine. [Quarantine rules](PROSPECTIVE_SYNTHETIC_PROTOCOL.md#custody-and-prediction-inputs)
2. Check syntax and shared comparator coverage before spending time on counting builds. Independently check both raw single-site positives, one reversible sham of the same growth order, and an appropriate superlinear control. A control specification can require streaming one output for every ordered pair, which entails quadratic output item work even in a scalar program. Review the relation of each slot's problem, mechanism, and authoring origin to the other slots in the family. [Source-domain preflight](SYNTHETIC_STUDY_RUNBOOK.md#before-evaluation)
3. Record the counts of proposed origins, rights and provenance rejections, domain failures, complete four-slot submissions, output or sanitizer failures, growth or reference failures, and final connected families. Verify the complete three-seed, six-size grid and preserve terminal reasons. Register actual primary quotas, controls, and growth bands ahead of the separate draw. The primary design needs at least 60 confirmed positive families from 80 drawn, with secondary coverage measured separately. The candidate list gives no estimate of that retention rate.
