# WP-A4 conclusions

This table compares the previous manuscript, releases and audit with the
[recomputed receipt](grouped_statistics.json). Bootstrap limits below are
descriptive 95% whole-cluster percentile limits, not tests. “CP” means the
two-sided 95% Clopper–Pearson binomial reference interval. Rates and intervals
not repeated here are in the receipt with their denominators. P-values are
two-sided; secondary p-values are nominal, not Holm-adjusted.
